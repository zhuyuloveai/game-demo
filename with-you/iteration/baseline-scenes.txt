export function createMoment(before,after,id){const scene=places[id];const visits=(before.sceneVisits?.[id]||0);const changes=[];const money=after.cash-before.cash;if(money)changes.push(`余额 ${money>0?'+':'−'}¥${Math.abs(money)}`);const energy=id==='rest'?Math.min(30,100-before.energy):id==='work'?-18:-12;changes.push(`精力 ${energy>=0?'+':''}${energy}`);if(id==='study')changes.push('能力 +1');if(before.slot===1)changes.push('结束后夜间恢复精力 +12（上限 100）');return {id,day:before.day,slot:before.slot,text:scene.lines[visits%scene.lines.length],changes};}
