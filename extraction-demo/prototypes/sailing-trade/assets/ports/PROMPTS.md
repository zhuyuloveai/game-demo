# Original port background art

These three original fictional port illustrations were generated with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. They are environment assets for the sailing-and-trade prototype, not screenshots, maps, characters, or recovered art from any existing game. No source/reference images were supplied. The final PNG files were copied into this directory without image editing or resizing; the original generated files were preserved.

The intended shared layout places trading facilities on the left, an inn at the back-left, a shipyard on the right, and quiet quay paving along the bottom for separately rendered game controls. Visual review confirmed that all three assets share this layout, contain no UI or readable text, and provide distinct warm southern, cool northern, and sandstone-port settings.

```text

The buildings and paths should read clearly at game-screen size.
Composition/framing: Landscape 16:9 composition, full-bleed picture covering the entire canvas. A fixed view from a slightly elevated person-height position on the stone-paved quay, looking into the harbor town; architectural perspective, not a top-down map. The central open quay leads toward the sea. Place a clearly recognizable merchants' guild and trading arcade on the left around 25% of image width, a tavern/inn farther behind it at the back-left around 40%, and a recognizable shipyard/drydock on the right around 80%. Each building has a clear visible entrance and distinct silhouette so it can be a clickable game destination. Foreground crates, folded trading fabrics and coiled ropes sit along the sides. Calm harbor water, mountainous islands, and historical sailing vessels fill the distant center. Reserve the bottom 10% for a naturally calm continuation of paving with low visual detail, leaving room for game controls that will be added separately. No blank border or artificial panel.
Constraints: Original fictional port, historically inspired sailing-ship era. No letters, no numbers, no writing, no labels, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. No large portraits or foreground character closeups. No modern objects, guns, cars, steamships, aerial map, split-screen, panels, or collage. Do not copy any existing game's map, character, or screenshot.
Primary request: DAWN PORT (the location name is context only, do not write it in the image). A warm southern-European-inspired trading harbor in soft golden morning light. Cream and white plaster walls, red terracotta roof tiles, elegant stone trading arches, wooden shutters, and bustling trade conveyed by a modest number of tiny distant dockworkers and carefully arranged cargo. The left-side merchants' guild has a broad arched entrance and trade goods beneath its veranda. The back-left inn has a welcoming shaded terrace. The right-side shipyard shows an open workshop and a wooden sailing vessel under repair beside a small stone drydock. Rich blue-green harbor water, distant green mountainous islands, ocher paving, warm sunlit sails. Bright, fresh, prosperous, and adventurous; detailed but readable.
```
