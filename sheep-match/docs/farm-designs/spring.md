Background natural land and water ONLY; do not add trees, buildings, bridge, fences, flowers, animals, floating clouds, sun, stars, UI or text because all are later separate unlockable stickers. Make this a fresh lovely location rather than original central S-path scene.

Transparent PNG landscape 4:3, exact 4 columns x 3 rows of equal square cells, every item centered in its own cell with generous transparent padding; absolutely no overlap or items crossing cell edges. Exact order: row1 pink blossoming CHERRY TREE, tiny daisy grass clump, tiny smiling gold star, soft fluffy spring cloud; row2 cheerful sunrise sun, small wooden FOOTBRIDGE over a short narrow blue creek segment, adorable small white RABBIT with pink cheeks, pastel BUTTERFLY; row3 little cream cottage with pink gable roof and a blue door, low pale wooden picket fence, cottage-garden flowerbox with pink/yellow tulips, round tiny blue songbird. No labels, text, UI, background or extra objects. Each complete silhouette entirely inside its cell; strictly transparent gutters. Spring storybook sticker style, no photorealism.
